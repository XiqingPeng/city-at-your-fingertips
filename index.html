Manual mode

Lift the skyline

  1. Show one open hand. Hold it flat like a tray, tilted slightly toward the camera.
  2. Lift in short strokes to grow buildings. Bring your hand back down right away between lifts.
  3. Pause for half a second, then press down to lower buildings. Bring your hand back up right away to repeat. Hold still to keep the height.
